Import from your broker

Download your holdings as a CSV file from your broker's website, then choose it here. We look for columns such as ticker or symbol, quantity, and average or book cost. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Back up or move to another device

Your holdings live only in this browser, so clearing its data deletes them. Keep a backup file, or open a restore link on your phone or another computer. Both include your holdings, watchlist, saved screens and model portfolios.
