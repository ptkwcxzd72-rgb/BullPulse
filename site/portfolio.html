A model for research and learning, not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell. Past performance is hypothetical: it uses today's choice of stocks and weights on the last year of prices, in each stock's own currency, with no dealing costs, taxes or currency moves. Risk figures come from the last year of daily prices and will change.
