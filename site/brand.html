02

Construction

The mark is a single line. Two horns frame a heartbeat, and the heartbeat's spike rises higher than the horns: the bull and the pulse in one stroke.

03

Usage

Leave clear space around the logo equal to half the mark's height. Never set the full logo smaller than 120 pixels wide on screen; below that, use the mark alone, down to 16 pixels.

✓ Use the original files

Use the SVGs as supplied, scaled in proportion.

✕ Don't stretch it

Never change the proportions or redraw the wordmark.

✕ Don't recolour it

Use only the primary or one-colour versions.

✕ Don't place it on busy colour

Use Paper, white or Midnight backgrounds.

04

06

Voice

BullPulse sounds like a careful analyst, not a tipster. It says what the data shows, how sure it is, and where the limits are. Three rules: plain words, evidence first, never hype.

SituationSayNot
Describing the scoreRanks stocks on trend, momentum and newsPredicts the next winners
A strong stockSignals mostly positiveStrong buy
Possible pricesA typical range over six monthsPrice target

Data problemsPrices missing between 3 and 12 JuneHide it, or "minor issues"
07

Motion

Motion should feel like a steady heartbeat: the line draws itself once, then beats slowly. Nothing flashes or bounces. When a visitor asks for reduced motion, everything holds still.

Draw-on
1.8 s, ease-in-out, once on load
Beat
glow every 2.4 s, peaks at 12%, gone by 30%
Pulse line
teal trace, about 55 px a second, one heartbeat every 960 px
Reveal
