Model portfolios are produced by the same fixed rules for every reader. They don't take account of anyone's circumstances, aims or attitude to risk, and they are not a recommendation to buy or sell any investment. Backtests show how rules would have done in the past, with assumptions about costs; they are not a promise of future returns, and real results can be worse. The value of shares can fall as well as rise and you may get back less than you invest. BullPulse is a free research tool, not a regulated adviser.
