Portfolio generator

Choose an aim and your limits. The generator picks UK and US shares from tonight's figures, keeps to your limits, explains every choice, works out whole shares for your amount and shows how the same rules did since 2017. It runs in your browser; your settings and holdings stay on this device.

1What do you want the portfolio to do?

Each aim is a published recipe of the signals BullPulse calculates. The badge says how that recipe did against an index fund since 2017, after costs.

2Your limits

These are rules for the portfolio, not questions about you; the generator doesn't judge whether investing suits your circumstances.

The generator applies the same published rules for everyone who chooses the same settings. It doesn't take account of your circumstances, aims or attitude to risk, and its output isn't a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Backtests show how rules would have done in the past, with assumptions about costs; they don't promise future returns. Shares can fall as well as rise and you may get back less than you invest. BullPulse is a free research tool, not a regulated adviser.
